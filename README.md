# Heart of Live (Totem of Undying)

---
[![Showcase](https://img.youtube.com/vi/fUsHPUQsmaE/maxresdefault.jpg)](https://youtu.be/fUsHPUQsmaE)

The **Heart of Live** is an item that can prevent players or mobs from dying when they take fatal damage and have it equipped in the utility Slot.

Instead of death, the Item will be consumed and the player will be healed.

It can be crafted in the Arcanist`s Workbench from 1 Voidheart and 10 Greater Essence of Life.
